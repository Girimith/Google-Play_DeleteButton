Delete your account and data

This page explains how to request deletion of your Daily Pocket account, and what happens to your data afterward.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the Daily Pocket app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings. Tap your profile icon, then select Settings.
  3. Select "Delete Account." Tap Account, then Delete Account, near the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm your request. Enter your password (or confirm via the on-screen prompt) and tap Delete My Account to submit the request.
